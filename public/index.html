🏠 Your house, in VR

Scan rooms with your Meta Quest, let Claude detect the furniture, then walk through your house from any device.

My houses

🥽 Scan a room

  1. For the most accurate scan (Quest 3 / 3S): stand in the room. Inside the scan you can press Room setup to let the headset map walls, floor and furniture. If you already ran Space Setup for this room, it's reused.
  2. Optional — let Claude see the room: enable the headset camera, then press Photo while looking at furniture (3–6 photos is great).
  3. Press Save room. Claude detects the furniture and the room appears in your house.

No room data on your headset? Use Trace floor and click each corner of the floor with your controller or a pinch.
